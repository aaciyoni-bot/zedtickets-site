How ZedTickets Works

STEP 1

Create

An organizer lists an event or opens a fundraiser in minutes.

STEP 2

Pay with MoMo

Buyers and donors pay in Kwacha — no card, no account needed.

STEP 3

Get your QR

A digital ticket with a unique QR lands on the phone instantly.

STEP 4

Scan at the gate

The organizer scans each QR once — no double entry, no fakes.

Transparent by design

Every payment is recorded with a receipt. For fundraisers, each donation is logged and receipted so causes and their supporters can see exactly what was raised. ZedTickets charges a small platform fee per transaction — always shown up front.
